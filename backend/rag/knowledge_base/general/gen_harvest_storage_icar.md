# ICAR-CIPHET Post-Harvest Management and Storage Guidelines

## 1. General Principles of Crop Harvest Timing
- Never harvest during rainfall or when foliage is covered with morning dew. Excess moisture accelerates microbial decay, heating in transit, and mould formation.
- Harvest during the coolest part of the day (early morning) to minimize field heat in harvested produce.

## 2. Pre-Cooling and Field Heat Removal
- For perishable horticulture crops (tomato, leafy vegetables, fruits), removing field heat within 2–4 hours of harvest extends shelf life by 30–50%.
- Keep harvested crates in shaded, well-ventilated packing areas away from direct sunlight.

## 3. Standard Optimal Storage Temperature and Humidity Matrix
- **Onion**: 25°C–30°C, 65%–70% RH (Ventilated Chawl) or 0°C–2°C (Cold Storage).
- **Potato**: 8°C–10°C, 85%–90% RH (Table); 10°C–12°C (Processing).
- **Tomato**: 10°C–12°C (Ripe), 12°C–15°C (Breaker), 85%–90% RH.
- **Wheat & Grains**: Ambient dry, <12% seed moisture, <65% RH in sealed metal bins.
- **Pulses & Soybean**: Ambient dry, <10% seed moisture in airtight hermetic bags.

## 4. Farm-Gate Grading and Packaging Standards
- Grade produce into Uniform Sizes: Grade A (Large/Premium), Grade B (Medium), Grade C (Small/Damaged).
- Mixing diseased or over-ripe produce with Grade A lots drastically reduces the entire batch's market valuation at APMC auctions.
